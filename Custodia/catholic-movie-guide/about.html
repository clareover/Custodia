About This Guide

Why this resource exists

Catholic Movie Guide is being built to serve Catholic families and individuals who want to engage with film and television thoughtfully, in a way that respects the moral teaching of the Church and the dignity of the human person.

Our Purpose

Many existing parents’ guides focus primarily on counting instances of language, sexuality, and violence. While those details matter, a fully Catholic approach also asks deeper questions:

  • What does this story say about God, the human person, sin, grace, and redemption?
  • How are marriage, family, the Church, and the sacraments treated?
  • Does the film ultimately lead the viewer toward truth and virtue, or away from them?

We aim to provide both the practical details (including timestamps) and the moral-theological context that helps viewers form sound judgments.

Current Status

This site is in early development. The design, structure, and rating framework are in place. Detailed film reviews and timestamped content notes will be added over time. Thank you for your patience as the resource grows.

Independence

This is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or officially connected to the United States Conference of Catholic Bishops, any particular diocese, or any other Catholic organization. The guidance offered here is advisory only.

Contact & Contributions

As the project develops, opportunities for feedback and carefully moderated contributions may be added. For now, the focus remains on building a solid, faithful foundation.
