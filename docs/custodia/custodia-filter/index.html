Library Guide

Watch with peace of mind

Choose a movie, set your filters, and Custodia will skip or mute the parts you prefer to avoid — using your own authorized copy.

Available Filters

How it works

Select a movie → turn filters on or off → open the player. During playback Custodia skips or mutes the segments you chose.

Your content

Custodia never hosts or streams movies. It only works with copies you already have the right to watch (streaming subscription or disc).

Catholic lens

Categories include language & blasphemy, violence, sexual content, substance use, and other concerns informed by Catholic moral teaching.

Custodia is an independent project. Filters are advisory. Not affiliated with ClearPlay or any studio.
